SECTION 01 • PUBLIC & SCIENTIFIC NOTICE

Official Disclaimers

LAST UPDATED: OCT 26, 2026 (v3.1)
DISCLAIMER 1.1 NON-AFFILIATION NOTICE

Independent Observatory & Trademark Notice

The Doomsday Clock Live Global Threat & History Observatory is an independent educational, scientific-visualization, and open-source intelligence platform. While this site displays and cites the official historical Doomsday Clock readings (1947–2026, currently 85 seconds to midnight) set by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists, this website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists.

DISCLAIMER 1.2 DUAL-CLOCK DISTINCTION

Official Annual Reading vs. Autonomous ODC-LIVE v3.0 Engine

This platform operates two distinct clocks: (1) The Official Bulletin Doomsday Clock, which reflects the annual January consensus of the Bulletin's Science and Security Board (`85.00s`), and (2) Our proprietary Autonomous Live Data-Driven Doomsday Clock (`ODC-LIVE v3.0`), an experimental mathematical model that moves forward and backward in real time based on live news, USGS seismic feeds, and NASA EONET hazard streams. The `ODC-LIVE` reading is an algorithmic risk visualization, not an official pronouncement of the Bulletin.

DISCLAIMER 1.3 SIMULATION & MODELING

Policy Sandbox, ICBM Flight & Nuclear Winter Simulators

The interactive tools on this platform—including the "What Moves the Clock?" Policy Simulator, the Ballistic Missile Flight & Alert Window Calculator, the 1983 TTAPS Nuclear Winter City Visualizer, and the Birth Year Time Machine—are simplified educational and mathematical models designed to illustrate strategic decision compression, atmospheric physics, and diplomatic history. They do not constitute classified military intelligence, civil-defense instructions, or emergency weather alerts.

DISCLAIMER 1.4 LIVE SENSOR TELEMETRY

Real-Time Open API Feeds & Offline Fallbacks

Live data feeds (Wikipedia MediaWiki API, NASA EONET v3, USGS GeoJSON Earthquake Feed, and Open-Meteo) are fetched directly by your browser from public third-party endpoints. Network latency, rate limits, or upstream sensor revisions may occur. When offline or rate-limited, the observatory automatically switches to its verified built-in 2026 baseline archive.

SECTION 03 • TERMS OF USE & LICENSING

Terms and Conditions

Guidelines governing use of the observatory, embeddable widgets, downloadable PNG cards, and open-source datasets.

1. Permitted Educational, Journalism & Research Use

You are free to explore, cite, screenshot, download PNG status cards, and embed the live observatory widget (via our provided <iframe> snippet) on blogs, news sites, educational portals, and social media, provided that you retain visible attribution to the Doomsday Clock Live Observatory and do not misrepresent simulated settings (such as Policy Sandbox projections or `ODC-LIVE` real-time fluctuations) as official annual announcements of the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists.

2. Open Data & Third-Party Cartographic Attribution

Historical timeline extracts from Wikipedia are used under Creative Commons CC-BY-SA 4.0. Global country boundary vectors (`world-atlas@2/countries-110m.json`) originate from public-domain Natural Earth cartography rendered via open-source BSD-licensed D3.js and TopoJSON libraries. Seismic and satellite hazard streams are public-domain feeds provided by the USGS and NASA.

3. Acceptable Use & API Citizenship

Users and automated scrapers must not abuse the client-side refresh controls to flood upstream public science APIs (Wikipedia, NASA EONET, USGS, Open-Meteo) with excessive automated polling beyond the observatory's standard 30-minute background sync interval.

4. "As-Is" Provision & Limitation of Liability

This platform and all associated simulations, mathematical threat models, and live telemetry feeds are provided strictly "as is" without warranties of any kind. In no event shall the authors or contributors be liable for any decisions or actions taken in reliance on the simulated projections or real-time feeds displayed on this site.
